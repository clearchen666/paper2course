# Notice

Paper2Course is adapted from Zara Zhang's `codebase-to-course` project:

https://github.com/zarazhangrui/codebase-to-course

The original project provided the foundation for the interactive course format, including the warm developer-notebook design direction, scroll-based course structure, quiz patterns, group chat/data-flow interactions, glossary tooltips, and code-to-English translation block system.

Paper2Course modifies and extends that foundation for AI academic papers, including:

- paper acquisition and analysis workflow
- AI-paper-specific teaching heuristics
- bilingual English/Chinese course output
- paper-focused formula/table/algorithm translations
- final synthesis and FAQ sections
- research-reading oriented skepticism and encouragement

Credit and thanks to Zara Zhang for the original `codebase-to-course` work.
